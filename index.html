What Vantage actually does on every conversation

The same five moves, every time — the ones your best closer already makes, run by an agent that never has an off day.

01

Engage

The agent responds the instant a prospect shows up, opening with a hook pulled from what they sell to and who they are — not a generic greeting.

02

Qualify

It asks the three to five questions your reps always ask, and scores fit and intent as the answers come in — live, not after the call.

03

Present

It pulls only the proof points, pricing, and use cases that match what this prospect just told it — never the full deck, never in the wrong order.

04

Handle objections

Pushback gets met with your actual rebuttals, trained from your own call transcripts — and escalated to a human the moment it should be.

05

Close

It books the call, sends the contract, or hands a hot lead to a rep with the full transcript attached — the moment the prospect is ready.
